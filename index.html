Who we are

The standard, not diluted

"Their Principles is the community for 18 to 25 year olds, and only 18 to 25 year olds — built to return an ambitious generation back to what really matters."

Our generation's development was shaped by the pandemic, but the deeper problem runs further than that: human nature itself — the tendency to fall into distraction, get carried away by society, and absorb toxic limiting patterns and norms. We exist to fix both.

Nobody else combines these two things: genuine focus on the professional formation of ambitious individuals, and a room that is only ever people our own age. No older members diluting the standard. We help every member achieve their goals, chase their dreams, and fulfill their purpose professionally — while inevitably becoming a wise, centered, grounded, hard-working individual personally.

Most rooms our members have been in are either too old, too surface-level, or too motivational without substance. What shifts everything is realizing: it isn't about knowing what to do — it's about needing the room, the people, and the accountability, people your exact age and exact stage, to actually become it.

What membership looks like

The Events

Curated, in-person experiences built for depth of connection — never transactional networking. Every gathering is designed the same way: small enough that everyone actually talks to everyone.

Padel

Padel

Rotating pairs, a full afternoon at Padel X, and no way to spend two hours on a court without actually getting to know the person across the net. We're working toward a monthly cadence — the kind of standing plan you start rearranging your week around.

Dinners

Dinners at Cecconi's

A fixed table at Cecconi's, Soho House Miami — no name tags, no elevator pitches, just a long dinner with people who happen to be building something too. The conversations that start here tend to keep going long after the check comes.

Backgammon

Backgammon Nights

Low-key evenings with the Miami Backgammon Society — no agenda, no pitch, just a board, a drink, and enough time for a real conversation to happen without anyone forcing it.

Workshops

Workshops & Mentorship

Structured sessions on discipline, business, and personal growth, alongside one-on-one mentorship for members figuring out their next real decision — not a webinar, a room of people who will actually follow up with you.
