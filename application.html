Membership Is by Application Only

Application

Every application is read personally. If it's a fit, you'll hear from us directly.

Eligibility

Membership is for 18 to 25 year olds, and only 18 to 25 year olds. That boundary is the community; it is not flexible.

The process

Submit the application. We review it personally, and if it's a fit we reach out to talk. A few seats at each gathering are held for new applicants, so you may meet the room before you join it.

What's expected

Show up, in person. Bring real goals. Hold the standard for the people around you the way they hold it for you.

Two ways in

If a current member referred you or you hold an invitation code, use the invitation path. If not, introduce yourself; the second door leads to the same room.
